## F-1 [HIGH] The crossing gate is evaluated at a different price than the fill, so a maker can enter inside the margin band and be cleared at the raw mid

**Lines.** `would-take-as-x` 1051-1064, `would-take-as-y` 1066-1079; folds `live-bid-fold` 1009-1028, `live-offer-fold` 1030-1049;
`token-y-limit-at` 353-359, `token-x-limit-at` 361-367; gate sites 1205, 1348, 1582, 1617, 1655, 1690, 1763, 1839;
settlement basis 2852 (`settle-clearing-price` := `oracle-price`, the **raw** mid), roll filters 1960-1975 and 2004-2019.

`would-take-as-X` takes **one** `price` and uses it for two different jobs:

```
(and (> price u0)
     (>= price limit)                                     ;; 1. is the ENTRANT's limit crossed?
     (get found (fold live-bid-fold … { price: price, … })))   ;; 2. is a COUNTERPARTY fillable at that price?
```

and all eight gate sites pass the **widened** mid (`widen-down price` for the y side, `widen-up price` for the x side).
Job 1 wants the widened price; job 2 must be asked at the price the fill will actually use, which is the raw mid —
settlement sets `settle-clearing-price` to `oracle-price` (2852) and decides every roll against it (1961, 2005), never against a widened one.

**Impact.** Resting orders in the 40-bp band are invisible to the gate but do fill:

* enter an **ask**: bids with effective limit in `[clearing, widen-up(clearing))` are not seen at 1.004·clearing;
* enter a **bid**: offers with effective limit in `(widen-down(clearing), clearing]` are not seen at 0.996·clearing.

The entrant's own limit test (job 1) is satisfied for any strictly crossing order, so a crossing order is admitted as a
maker whenever the counter side sits in that band. The documented invariant —
*"A resting order cannot sit at mid without paying the taker rebate, pegged or not"* (`README-markets-v6-pegged.md`:139) — does not hold.

**Repro (exact integers; `PRICE_PRECISION = 1e8`, `MAKER_MARGIN_BPS = 40`, mid = `100000000`).**
Race-free, one cycle:

1. Bids rest as fixed orders with `limit-price` = `100100000`, `100200000`, `100300000` (all ≥ mid; all fillable at mid).
2. Seller calls `deposit-token-x(amount, limit-price = 99900000, spread-bps = none, update, t, name)` on a print whose mid is `100000000`.
   Gate: `widen-up(mid) = 100400000 ≥ 99900000` → true. Counter-side fold at `100400000`: needs a bid with
   `100400000 ≤ limit`, and the highest is `100300000` → `found = false` → `would-take-as-x` = **false** → no `u1016`.
   The ask rests at `99900000`, i.e. through the mid, without paying anything.
3. Anyone calls `settle-with-refresh` with a print whose mid is `100000000`.
   `filter-limit-violating-token-x-depositor` rolls on `clearing < limit` → `100000000 < 99900000` is false → the ask stays;
   the three bids roll on `clearing > limit` → false → stay. The ask is cleared pro-rata **at the mid `100000000`** — 10 bp
   above the limit it rested at, through the mid, having funded no rebate and having never been told to use `swap`.

**Fix.** Two prices, one function signature — job 1 at the widened mid, job 2 at the raw mid:

```
(define-read-only (would-take-as-x (raw uint) (limit uint))
  (and (> raw u0)
       (>= (widen-up raw) limit)                               ;; entry margin, unchanged
       (get found (fold live-bid-fold (get-token-y-depositors (var-get current-cycle))
                        { price: raw, found: false }))))       ;; the price the batch will clear at
```

with the mirror for y. This is what the roll filters already do (they pass `clearing`), so the two sides of the contract
stop disagreeing about what "crossing" means.

## F-2 [MEDIUM] The fixed 40-bp margin is under-priced above age 50 s — the stated calibration holds only at the base of the curve

**Lines.** `MAKER_MARGIN_BPS u40` (5), `rebate-bps-for-age` 20-31, `REBATE_GRACE_SECS u30` (7), `MAX_STALENESS u80` (29),
freshness asserts 998-999.

Exact values of the shipped curve: `b(age) = 20` for `age ≤ 30`; `b(age) = age − 10` for `31 ≤ age ≤ 79`; `b(80) = 70` (unreachable, see F-3).

| age | 30 | 40 | **50** | 51 | 60 | 79 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `b(age)` bp | 20 | 30 | **40** | 41 | 50 | 69 |

`b(50) = 40 = MAKER_MARGIN_BPS`, `b(51) = 41 > 40`, `b(79) = 69`. The design states the margin "exceeds the 20 bp base
rebate deliberately"; 40 > 20 is true only at the base, while the accepted window is 80 s and the curve crosses the margin at
**age 50 s**. For every print older than that, the taker price of the same execution exceeds the maker-side margin, and the
gap grows 1 bp per second to 29 bp — over a third of the window is mispriced in the direction that favours the dodge.

**Why the comparison matters.** The two paths differ in exactly one economic term: the maker path funds no rebate.
Both pay the same `FEE_BPS` 10 per side once (batch: `token-y-fee`/`token-x-fee` 2866-2867 paid to the treasury; walk:
`execute-fill` 2205-2240). The rebate is carved out of the taker's own amount and smeared over the clearing side pro-rata
(`ride-x/ride-y` 2904-2911, `settle-token-*-after-fee` 2925-2931, remainder dropped 2932-2933), so a taker who clears alone
gets their own rebate back and a taker who shares the side hands `b(age)·(1 − own share)` to the co-clearing makers.
So **C, "is 40 over 20 enough": at ages ≤ 50 s the stated argument stands; above 50 s it does not** — the rebate the same
fill would have paid is strictly larger than the margin the gate demands, and where F-1 makes the two fills identical,
the payoff is exactly `b(age)` (41…69 bp). Sequence: (i) take a print at age `a > 50`; (ii) enter through F-1's band (or,
if F-1 is fixed, at 40 bp + ε); (iii) settle at/through the limit. No hidden claim: with F-1 fixed the entrant must earn a
40-bp favourable move, which is the design's own calibration assumption; **that assumption is what the table prices, and it
is only valid to age 50**.

**Fix.** Price the entry against the same curve, not a constant: `deposit-token-x/y` already fetch the print through
`fresh-classification-price` (1006-1008), which **throws away the age** `fresh-classification-price-aged` just computed
(979-1005). Keep it and widen by the charged bps:

```
(widen-up-by price (max MAKER_MARGIN_BPS (rebate-bps-for-age age)))
```

one new helper (`widen-*` with a bps argument, 14-21), four call sites (1205, 1348, 1582, 1617, 1655, 1690, 1763, 1839).

## F-3 [LOW] The curve's ceiling is unreachable and its ≥80 clamp is dead code

`rebate-bps-for-age` returns `TAKER_REBATE_MAX_BPS = 70` for `age ≥ 80` (26-28), but the only producer of an `age` that
reaches a fee path asserts `(> (get publish-time feed-x) (- stacks-block-time MAX_STALENESS))` (998-999) — **strict `>`** —
so `age ≤ 79` and the maximum rebate actually payable is `b(79) = 69`. The documented curve ("one bp per second to
`TAKER_REBATE_MAX_BPS` (70) at 80 s") is one bp short of its own ceiling, and the `≥ 80` branch cannot execute on any of the
four call paths (2184, 2237, 2245, 2245 / 1739, 1813). Impact: a 1-bp mispricing at the top of the window, and a guard that
will never fire — including in the case it looks written for (a future relaxation of the freshness assert to `>=`).
Fix: `(>= age MAX_STALENESS)` → `(>= age (- MAX_STALENESS u1))`, or drop the branch and document 69 as the ceiling.

## N-1 [INFO] The widening is a silent no-op below mid 250

`widen-up`/`widen-down` (14-21) use `(/ (* price MAKER_MARGIN_BPS) BPS_PRECISION)`, which truncates to `0` for
`price < 250`. Below that mid the margin vanishes and the gate degenerates into a strict-crossing test. Not reachable for
`sBTC/STX` at today's scale (mid ≈ px/py·1e8 ≈ 5·10¹²) — flagged because the same file is the template for other markets
(`markets-sbtc-usdcx-*`), where a two-feed ratio below 2.5·10⁻⁶ is conceivable. Fix: `(max u1 …)` on the widening.

## Checked clean, with the denominator

**A — the curve.** Continuous and monotone at both boundaries (`b(30)=20`, `b(31)=21`; `b(79)=69`, `b(80)=70`); no rounding
in bps (all integer arithmetic, `BPS_PRECISION = 10000`); the clamp above 80 exists but is unreachable (F-3).
The age is charged on the **older** feed (`(oldest (if (< pub-x pub-y) pub-x pub-y))`, 988-1005) while the price uses both —
charging the older of the two is the conservative choice, and each feed is independently asserted fresh (998-999).
The bps carved at booking is what is used at fill time (`pending-rebate-bps-x/y`, 2237/2245 read at 2245-2265), so no
age drift between booking and fill: the curve is applied once, to the stored value.
**B/D — the eight gate sites and their directions.** 1205/1582/1655/1763 use `widen-down` for bids, 1348/1617/1690/1839 use
`widen-up` for asks. For a bid, both effects of the wider test push the same way — `widen-down(mid) ≤ limit` is harder to
satisfy than `mid ≤ limit`, and the fold at the lower price admits fewer counterparties — so the direction is *against*
the entrant at every site; the only defect is the price *basis* (F-1), not the sign.
**E — pegged orders.** `spread > 0` is documented to never clear in the batch (`README-markets-v6-pegged.md`:147-151); the
code confirms it and shows why: the roll filter asks `(> clearing (token-y-limit-at dep clearing))` (1961) and a peg returns
`clearing·(1 − s/10000)` (or `u0` when out of band) → rolled at every settlement; the ask mirror rolls on
`(< clearing clearing·(1 + s/10000))` (or `MAX_UINT`) → rolled. The walk (`sorted-asks`/`sorted-bids` 2679/2746 with the raw
clearing as `mid`, `walk-x/y-book-step` 2420/2567) is the only consumer, as documented. The gate's "same rule" claim for pegs
holds only conditionally on the counter side being visible: the peg is derived at the **raw** entry mid (1202) and tested at
the widened one, so a peg with `s ≤ 40` is refused exactly like a fixed order in the margin — but inherits F-1 in full, so a
1-bp peg (the most aggressive quote allowed by `valid-spread`, 371-375) is admitted whenever the counter side sits in the band.
`spread = 0` behaves as the README says (in-range ⇒ fills in the batch at mid; `s = 0` ⇒ the roll condition is false).
**E — the walk/priority interaction.** `walk-y-book-step` fills the makers the batch just rolled, at the maker's own derived
price (`execute-fill … price = l`, 2420-2434), while the batch pays pro-rata at the clearing (`distribute-to-*-depositor`
2953-2977) — consistent with the design's own split, and the reason a peg's fill price moves with the print.
**F — fees.** Charged once per side per settlement (`token-y-fee = clearing_y·10/10000` 2866, mirrored 2867; walk fees
2205-2240), so the maker/taker price difference is the rebate alone (used in F-2).
**F — design.** The repo already carries the structural fix this market approximates: `README-markets-v7-taker-anchor.md`
(v7 source in `contracts/aborted/`, fork-verified 86/86 + 39/39 on 2026-09-16) makes every order carry `placed-at` and skips
any order with `placed-at >= settle-at`, i.e. *no order is ever filled at a price from before the block it was written in*.
That is precisely the class F-1/F-2 try to approximate with a fixed margin. **Recommendation: back-port the anchor alone**
(a `placed-at` map written on deposit/set-limit/readmit/reprice, one skip in the two roll filters, the walk and the gate),
**not** the whole two-transaction shape: it keeps atomicity — no keeper, no user latency, no expiry — and it removes the
class rather than pricing it. Residually it leaves only the settler window v7 itself documents ("What remains": the settler
still chooses among prints under 80 s that are newer than the orders it touches).
